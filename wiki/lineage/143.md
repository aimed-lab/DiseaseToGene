## What changed after the run, plainly

Two axes were re-run on 16 Sep, from a laptop on VPN rather than the VM, and both are
recorded above as new entries (`r143-network-2`, `r143-safety-2`) rather than edits:

- **Network symbols.** Seventeen `Cxorfnn` genes had network rows under an upper-cased
  symbol and were invisible to the board. The loader now keeps the snapshot's spelling; the
  scores did not change. 0 orphan rows afterwards.
- **Safety coverage.** The July reference table was missing 1,473 of the symbols across the
  three current snapshots; 420 of them do have a gnomAD constraint record (PRRG1, ACE2, ABCB7
  among them) and were merged in from the live API. Coverage went from 5,259 to 5,434 genes;
  the remaining absences are genes gnomAD has no constraint for.

Still not perfect: **`source_url` is empty** on every row, as on every snapshot so far; the
badge's live link is a template built from the gene and the row.

## Why `annotation`, `literature` (PubMed) and `patents` have `axis: null`

They are stored as **context**, never scored. Annotation is what the protein is; PubMed
counts sit beside the Europe PMC counts that do score, because the two corpora disagree by
enough that scoring both made genes incomparable; patents measure commercial crowding, and
rewarding that would bias the funnel toward targets that are already owned.
